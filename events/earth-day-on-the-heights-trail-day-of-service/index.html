​Join ATOTB on a service day sponsored by Sandlot Engineering out on the Heights Trail to care for the hundreds of trees we’ve planted.

​We will be mulching, pruning, de-weeding, watering, measuring, and re-staking (where needed)

Thanks to our Spring 2026 Trail Maintenance Sponsor
